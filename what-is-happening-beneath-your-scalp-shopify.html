The Root Cause: DHT

One hormone is behind most of the thinning you see.

  • DHT shrinks your follicles. It binds to receptors and signals them to miniaturize over time.
  • Smaller follicles produce thinner strands. Eventually they stop producing visible hair altogether.
  • This happens beneath the surface. By the time you notice thinning, it has been active for months.
  • Shampoos cannot reach it. DHT is produced internally. Surface products treat symptoms only.
  • Hormonal shifts increase DHT activity. After 35, postpartum, menopause, and stress all accelerate it.

Part 4

Why Topicals Alone Fail

If surface products could fix this, you would not still be looking.

  • Shampoos clean your scalp. They cannot block a hormone produced inside your body.
  • Biotin supports hair structure. It does not address DHT. If thinning is DHT-driven, biotin alone will not stop it.
  • Hair oils support circulation. They do not stop the signal shrinking your follicles.
  • Most serums coat the strand. The follicle damage beneath the scalp continues unchecked.
  • The root cause is internal. Real solutions have to include internal support first.
The distinction: Internal support blocks DHT where it is produced. Topical support creates a healthy surface environment. You need both. But internal comes first.
