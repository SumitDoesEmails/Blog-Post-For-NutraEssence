Section 1

Morning: Your Daily Dose

This is the foundation. Get this part right and you're already ahead of 90% of people who buy a supplement and take it inconsistently.

With Your First Meal
The non-negotiable part of your day
  • Take 2 softgels with breakfast or your first full meal. Never on an empty stomach. The fat content in food helps your body absorb the pumpkin seed oil more effectively.
  • Drink a full glass of water with your dose. Hydration supports absorption and helps transport the active compounds to where they need to go. It also supports kidney and bladder function.
  • Wait 30 minutes before coffee or caffeine. Caffeine can interfere with nutrient uptake in the early absorption window. Take your softgels first, then enjoy your morning coffee.
  • Same time every day. The specific hour matters less than the consistency. Your body builds an internal rhythm when the dose arrives at the same time daily.
  • Keep the bottle visible. Next to the coffee maker. On the breakfast table. Beside your keys. Wherever you look first in the morning. Out of sight is out of routine.
Anchor it to a habit you already have. "After I start the coffee maker, I take my softgels." "After I sit down for breakfast, the bottle is right there." You don't need willpower. You need a trigger.
Section 2

Throughout the Day: What Supports Absorption

You took the dose. Now give your body what it needs to actually use it.

Hydration + Habits
Simple things that compound over time
  • Drink 6 to 8 glasses of water throughout the day. This supports bladder health, kidney function, and helps the bioavailable compounds in the formula reach your follicles. Dehydration slows everything down.
  • Do not hold your bladder for extended periods. This is especially important for women taking pumpkin seed oil. Consistent bathroom habits support the urinary health benefits of the formula.
  • Eat whole, nutrient-dense foods where possible. Lean protein, leafy greens, healthy fats. These provide the building blocks your body needs alongside what the softgels deliver.
  • Reduce processed foods and excess sodium. Both increase systemic inflammation. Inflammation works directly against what the formula is doing at the follicle level. Less inflammation means better internal conditions for hair growth.
  • Move your body for at least 20 minutes. Walking counts. Movement improves circulation, including blood flow to the scalp. Better circulation means the active compounds reach the follicles more efficiently.
Section 3

Evening: The Recovery Window

Your body does its deepest repair work while you sleep. Hormone regulation, cellular turnover, follicle recovery. Set the conditions for it.

Before Bed
Where the internal work happens
  • Massage your scalp for 2 to 3 minutes. Use gentle circular motions with your fingertips. This improves blood flow directly to the follicles. It costs nothing and takes less time than brushing your teeth.
  • Keep your scalp clean. Remove heavy styling products, dry shampoo buildup, and anything that blocks the follicle opening. A clean scalp is a functional scalp.
  • Avoid tight hairstyles overnight. Ponytails, braids, and clips that pull on the hair shaft cause mechanical stress on follicles that are already under DHT pressure. Let your hair rest while your body works.
  • Prioritize 7 to 8 hours of sleep. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Cortisol (the stress hormone that accelerates shedding) drops. This is when your body runs its internal repair cycle. Cutting sleep short interrupts all of it.
  • Use a silk or satin pillowcase. Less friction on the hair shaft. Less breakage. Less morning shedding on the pillow. A small change with visible impact.
Section 4

What to Avoid

Just as important as what you do. These habits quietly work against the formula and slow your results.

Cut These Out
Habits that undermine what the formula is doing
  • Skipping days. Consistency is the single biggest factor. Missing one day is fine. Missing 3 to 4 in a row interrupts the internal support your follicles need to rebuild. The compound effect works both ways.
  • Taking softgels on an empty stomach. You may feel nausea or discomfort. More importantly, fat-soluble nutrients absorb poorly without food. Always take with a meal.
  • Excessive heat styling. Blow dryers, flat irons, and curling wands at high heat cause surface damage that mimics shedding. You will confuse heat breakage with actual hair loss and misread your progress.
  • Crash diets and extreme calorie restriction. Your body prioritizes vital organs when nutrients are scarce. Hair is not a vital organ. Undereating triggers a shedding response that no supplement can override.
  • Expecting overnight results. The hair growth cycle takes time. You are not going to see a transformation in week one. The internal shift starts before the visible one. Stay with it.
  • Doubling up on missed days. If you forget a dose, just resume your normal schedule the next morning. Taking two doses at once does not compensate and can cause discomfort.
